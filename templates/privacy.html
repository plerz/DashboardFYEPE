Privacy Policy

DashFYEPE · Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DashFYEPE handles information when you use the service to connect and manage authorized social media accounts.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the permissions you authorize, the service may process account identifiers, profile information, account statistics, access tokens, refresh tokens, content metadata, captions, publishing status, and scheduling information needed to provide the requested features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, display account information and statistics, prepare and publish content, schedule posts, maintain publishing history, and operate the service.

3. Passwords

DashFYEPE does not request or store your social media account password. Authentication is handled through the supported authorization flow.

4. Tokens and Security

Access and refresh tokens are handled by the application backend and are used only to perform actions authorized by the account owner. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information.

5. Sharing

Information is transmitted to the connected platform only as necessary to provide the features you request and to perform actions you authorize. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and Deletion

Connected account information is retained while needed to provide the service. You can disconnect an account from DashFYEPE; account data associated with the connection may then be removed according to the application's data-retention process.

7. Your Choices

You control which accounts you connect and which publishing actions you authorize. You may disconnect connected accounts and stop using the service at any time.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the service administrator through the contact information provided by DashFYEPE.
